What changed from V1?

V1 mostly showed thin rings with transparent gaps. V2 keeps a faint blue disc between the rings, uses broader radial peaks, modulates their brightness with Waves, and adds masked Cellular highlights. The camera is also lower and closer to the reference.

Do this first in 3ds Max

  1. Open Water_Ripple_V2_Open_And_Render.max. It starts at frame40. Press F9.
  2. Choose Scripting → Run Script, then open Learning_Panel.ms from the same folder.
  3. Click 1. Broad bands only, then F9. Click 2. Add Waves, then F9. Click 3. Add Cellular, then F9.

The panel toggles existing map layers. It does not rebuild the scene. Leave it on stage3 to restore the full result.

Find the maps in Slate

Press M to open the Material Editor. Use Modes → Slate Material Editor if needed. Choose Material → Pick from Object, then click the ripple Plane. Expand its connected maps and double-click 02_Waves_SCREENSHOT to show its parameters.

Your first parameter: Phase

Select 02_Waves_SCREENSHOT. At frame40 its Phase is0. Try0.25, render, then0.5 and render. The Waves pattern should shift. Keep Auto Key OFF while experimenting; reopen the supplied file if you want to discard your changes.

Four names to understand

ParameterMeaning in this project
Waves → PhaseShifts the Waves pattern. It does not move the Plane.
Waves → AmplitudeChanges the pattern's contrast. It does not raise the mesh here: Waves is connected to the color branch, not Displacement.
Composite → Layer OpacityHow strongly this layer affects the combined map. This is different from the material's Opacity input.
Material → OpacityWhich parts of the Plane remain visible. White is visible; black is invisible.
